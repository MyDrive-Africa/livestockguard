The proposed boundary has been drawn to **avoid all identified occupied structures**. The exclusion zones (X1–X5) represent areas where current inhabitants have existing structures. These areas are:

If the council identifies additional structures not visible on satellite imagery, or if current inhabitants dispute the boundary, the Sibanyoni family is prepared to:
- Adjust the boundary further west/south
